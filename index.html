Order direct from the kitchens of Keralam.

No commission, no rider army, no company in the middle. See who's cooking around you, place your order here — and the kitchen that cooks it delivers it with its own people and keeps every rupee. This site takes nothing. It only introduces.

How it works
  1. Set where you are — we ask your browser once, or you pick your area.
  2. Build your order on the kitchen's page.
  3. Your order opens as a WhatsApp message, from you to the kitchen — nothing is stored anywhere in between.
  4. They cook, their own people deliver, and they keep all the money.
